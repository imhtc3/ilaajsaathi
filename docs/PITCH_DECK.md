# 5-slide pitch deck content

## 1. The problem
- "Doctor ne operation bola hai, par paise nahi hain."
- Families told they need an angioplasty, knee replacement or kidney surgery pay private prices or delay care, because nobody tells them about free consults, Ayushman Bharat cover or government hospitals nearby.
- In emergencies, minutes are lost reaching a hospital that can't treat the problem.

## 2. The solution: IlaajSaathi
- Speak the problem in Hindi or English, share location and budget.
- Emergency: routed to the nearest hospital with the right facility (cath lab, stroke care, trauma centre), with 108 and directions.
- Otherwise: free consult, Ayushman Bharat check, nearest government hospitals, cost comparison, generic medicines, doctor summary and reminder.

## 3. How the agent works
Understand → Safety gate → Emergency routing or Plan (LLM + guardrails) → Tools (Google Maps, schemes, costs, generics) → Act (PDF, reminder) → Deliver in the user's language. Every step visible in the app.

## 4. Impact
- Demo case: about Rs 1.9 lakh possible saving on angioplasty at a government hospital, plus around Rs 800 a month on medicines.
- Emergencies: the right facility first, with travel time.
- Free first opinion via eSanjeevani; second opinion before major procedures.

## 5. Scale and feasibility
- Works anywhere in India with Google Maps; free OpenStreetMap fallback; offline mode.
- Next: PM-JAY hospital list and package rates, ABHA records, WhatsApp voice bot, ambulance dispatch integration.
- Partners: 108 services, CSCs, ASHA workers, Jan Aushadhi Kendras.
- Responsible: no diagnosis, safety rules in code, minimal data kept.
