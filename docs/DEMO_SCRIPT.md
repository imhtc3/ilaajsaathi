# 2–3 minute demo video script

**0:00–0:20 — The problem (face to camera)**
"When a family hears 'angioplasty, 3 lakh rupees', they either borrow money or delay treatment. And in a heart
attack, people lose precious time going to a hospital that can't treat it. IlaajSaathi fixes both."

**0:20–1:00 — Emergency (screen)**
Click **Chest pain right now with sweating** (location is shared).
Show: "Go to [hospital], 24x7 cath lab, 11 min", Call 108, Directions, map, other hospitals with a cath lab.
"It doesn't stop at 'go to emergency'. It routes to the right facility, then shows how to pay for the treatment after."
Scroll to "After the emergency: affordable options".

**1:00–1:50 — Affordable path**
Click the heart blockage sample (angioplasty quoted Rs 3 lakh, budget Rs 30,000). Show:
the trace (understood, safety check, plan, tools), nearest government cardiology hospitals with travel time,
Ayushman Bharat check, the government vs private table, generic medicines, the doctor summary PDF and reminder.
Then the Hindi knee-replacement sample: reply in Hindi, 72-year-old flagged likely eligible for Ayushman Vay Vandana.

**1:50–2:20 — Agentic + tech**
Safety gate in code, Gemini planner with guardrails, free OpenStreetMap maps (Google Maps optional),
works even offline, one Docker image, `/invoke` API.

**2:20–2:40 — Impact and close**
"In this case: about Rs 1.9 lakh of possible savings on the angioplasty and around Rs 800 a month on
medicines. In an emergency: the right hospital, minutes faster. IlaajSaathi — kifayati, surakshit ilaaj ka raasta."
